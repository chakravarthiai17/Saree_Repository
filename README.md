# SareeHub – Premium Indian Sarees E-Commerce Platform

> **"Discover the Beauty of India, One Saree at a Time."**  
> *Explore Beautiful Handloom & Designer Sarees from Every Corner of India.*

---

## 🌟 Executive Overview

**SareeHub** is a luxury, mobile-responsive full-stack Indian ethnic fashion e-commerce destination. It connects connoisseurs and brides with authentic, master-woven sarees directly from heritage weaving clusters across 15+ Indian states and 23 distinct saree categories.

---

### 4. Product Listing & Filtering (`/shop`)
- Real-time multi-facet filters:
  - Price Range Slider / Inputs (₹0 – ₹50,000+)
  - State of origin filter
  - Saree category filter
  - Fabric filter (Mulberry Silk, Katan, Crepe, Jamdani, Tussar, Cotton, Linen, etc.)

- Sorting: Best Sellers, New Arrivals, Customer Ratings, Price Low to High, Price High to Low

### 5. Product Details Experience (`/product/[id]`)
- Multi-image zoom lens / hover magnifier
- Color hue selectors

- Working "Write a Review" form with dynamic rating updates
- "Add to Shopping Bag" and instant "Buy Now with 1-Click"
- Related saree recommendations from the same state/category

### 6. Cart & Secure Checkout
- Instant Slide-over **Cart Drawer** & dedicated **Cart Page** (`/cart`)
- Free delivery progress threshold (unlocked at ₹1,999)
- Promotional coupon engine (`UTSAV10`, `BRIDAL20`, `ROYAL500`, `HANDLOOM15`)
- Secure checkout form (`/checkout`) with address auto-fill
- **Payment Terminal Modal**:
  - UPI / QR scan simulation
  - Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay test mode)
  - Net Banking
  - Cash on Delivery (COD)
  - Integration-ready Razorpay sandbox
- **Order Success Page** (`/order-success/[id]`):
  - Confetti celebration
  - Printable invoice receipt
  - Live milestone delivery tracking timeline

### 7. Customer Account (`/account`)
- User registration, login, and instant one-click demo logins
- Profile management and saved address book
- **My Orders** (`/account/orders`) with tracking modals
- **My Wishlist** (`/account/wishlist`) with move-to-bag functionality

### 8. Admin Control Center (`/admin`)
- **Dashboard Overview** (`/admin`): Revenue stats, orders summary, low-stock weave alerts
- **Product Manager** (`/admin/products`):
  - Add new sarees with image preview and full specs
  - Edit existing sarees without touching source code
  - Delete sarees with confirmation
  - Stock management and badge toggling
- **Order Manager** (`/admin/orders`):
  - View customer addresses and ordered items
  - Update fulfillment statuses (Placed ➔ Processing ➔ Dispatched ➔ Delivered ➔ Cancelled)
  - Add timeline notes for customers
- **Categories & States Hub** (`/admin/categories`)
- **Coupons Manager** (`/admin/coupons`): Create and delete discount codes
